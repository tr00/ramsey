# Degree-18 further reductions

Date: 2026-10-14. Mathematical work only; no search or acquisitions.

Proved every four-clique Q in the 24-vertex antineighborhood has internal
degree sum at most 60. Equality forces degree 15 at each Q vertex and two
external vertices in each of the ten pair/triple Q signature classes. For
four size-four columns this bounds their total global excess above 18 by four;
equality makes all four vertices degree 19.

Proved an 84-edge (4,5)-free neighborhood has at most three individually
K4-safe missing edges; their graph is a union of stars with at most three
edges. Such a neighborhood is either already saturated or has at most three
single-edge augmentations to an extremal 85-edge graph.

Specified a safe root choice maximizing neighborhood edges among degree-18
vertices and exact interface inequalities transferring that bound to other
degree-18 vertices. The root-choice precondition is essential.

Updated results.md with the frontier band, small-column deductions, these
new results, and corrected complement selector tie coverage. No frontier
value or global Ramsey bound was improved. Full proofs:
docs/degree18_cliques_augmentation.md.
