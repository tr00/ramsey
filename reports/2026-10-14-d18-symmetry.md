The main design is to fix A, generate the sorted multiset of actual B
columns modulo Aut(A), and then complete the B graph modulo the multiset
stabilizer. This uses S_24 before B is fixed. It includes a proof that least
full sorted multisets have least sorted prefixes, allowing witnessed prefix
rejections without losing every representative of a valid rooted graph.

The exact residual group fits

```text
1 -> product_C S_(m_C) -> Gamma_M -> H_M -> 1,
H_M = the subgroup of Aut(A) preserving the column multiset.
```

The frontier-specific deductions are:

- For x=136..141, every full signature occurs at most twice. Repeated pairs
  are forced adjacent through x=139. At x=140,141 a repeated signature can
  have size at most ten, and adjacency is not forced by the union bound at
  size ten.
- At x=136, the kernel is (C2)^p, p<=12, and every duplicate pair is a
  forced edge. Thus its order is at most 4096. A sorted multiset represents
  at least 24!/4096 = 151476660579404160000 distinct labeled cross matrices

- Selected full-vector lex comparisons, including comparisons only against
  group generators, preserve coverage. They need not achieve complete orbit
  minimization. Combining partial comparisons requires a common surviving
  path proof; preserving arbitrary local edge domains alone is insufficient.
- If x=136+k and q=85-e(A), the inequality 3k-5q<48 implies n18>n24.
  Therefore all degree-18 candidates with x<=151 already satisfy the
