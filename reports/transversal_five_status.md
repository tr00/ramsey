# Transversal-Five Attempt Status

Date: 2026-10-13

The attempted strengthening

```text
tau_K4(F) >= 5
```

is not yet proved.

## Progress

- A hypothetical four-vertex transversal leaves a 14-vertex `(4,4)`-Ramsey
  core with 41, 42, or 43 edges.
- The 43-edge core is rigorously impossible.
- In the complementary 85-edge graph, only four attachment families remain:
  one 49-edge `C4` family and three types of 50-edge families.
- Triangle-deficit arguments eliminate all 48-edge cases and the 49-edge
  diamond case.
- Paw and diamond families have tight, explicitly recorded intersection
  structures.
- No explicit counterexample was found.

## Caveat

An intermediate argument incorrectly treated common nonneighbors of a
nonedge as a clique. The valid conclusion is only that their independence
number is at most 2. The associated apparent exclusion was discarded.

## Recommended continuation

Apply `K4` saturation of the extremal 85-edge graph to the four remaining
families. If that stalls, request permission to inspect the 74 published
extremal `(4,5,18)` graphs and independently verify their transversal numbers.
