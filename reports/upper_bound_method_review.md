# Upper-bound paper review

Date: 2026-10-14. Read arXiv:2409.15709v2 at the user's request.

The R(5,5)<=46 paper uses linear programming/subgraph counting, selected
edge-dense catalogues, a coverage theorem for pointed-neighborhood pairs, and
two independent gluing computations. It does not present SDP or flag-algebra
filtering. The key extension needed for order 43 is an unavoidable-family
theorem, beyond the current filters at a chosen frontier root.

Source version, hash, section-level account and order-43 implications:
docs/upper_bound_method_review.md. No new Ramsey bound or computational
exclusion is claimed.
