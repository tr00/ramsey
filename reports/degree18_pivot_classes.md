# Degree-18 pivot classification report

Date: 2026-10-14. Mathematical deductions with a small exact rational-arithmetic
table check; no graph enumeration, solver, downloads or installations.

Classified numerical degree-18 root profiles by neighborhood deficit q and
neighbor-degree multiplicities. Global degree excess W fixes
b=148+W/2-q-E and x=136+2q+E. Census bounds give
max(0,W/2-12-E)<=q<=min(35,W/2+4-E).

Proved every x=136..138 pivot has an adjacent degree-18 root with codegree
at most nine; throughout x=136..141 at most ten suffices. The general
profile-dependent formula uses E(4,5,17)=79 from the reviewed paper.

For W=0,2,4,6,8,10, every degree-18 root has respectively a degree-18
neighbor with overlap at most 9,9,10,11,12,12, and both neighborhoods have
at least 81-W/2 edges. These are complete conditional pointed-pair reductions.

Weighted counts of excess incident to degree-18 roots additionally force
adjacent roots with at least 81 neighborhood edges for W=2 or4, and at least
80 for W=6. Those pairs need not coincide with the small-overlap pairs.

The unrestricted degree-18 branch is not reduced to small k or small W.
The excess identity alone loses q after substituting the degree profiles;
additional structural inequalities are needed. No branch was excluded.
